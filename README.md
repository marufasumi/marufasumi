## 👩‍💻 About Me

I am an **AI Data Engineer** with a background spanning pharmaceutical quality control, scientific research, enterprise Salesforce development, data engineering, analytics, and machine learning.

My career began in pharmaceutical laboratories, where I worked with **HPLC, GC, UV–Vis, FT-IR, dissolution testing, GMP documentation, assay validation, drug stability studies, and regulatory quality processes**. That experience gave me a strong foundation in data integrity, compliance, traceability, scientific reasoning, and evidence-based decision-making.

Today, I apply that same rigor to building **AI-ready data platforms, healthcare analytics systems, machine learning applications, Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems, and enterprise data solutions**.

My work and academic projects focus on:

- Scalable data engineering with **Databricks, PySpark, Delta Lake, SQL, and Azure**
- Healthcare data engineering and analytics
- AI applications for pharmacology, pharmacovigilance, and clinical research
- HL7 FHIR–based healthcare interoperability
- Machine learning and statistical modeling
- Salesforce Data Cloud, Health Cloud, Agentforce, Apex, and LWC
- Production-oriented AI applications using LangChain, ChromaDB, Streamlit, and REST APIs

I am especially interested in building systems that connect **data engineering, analytics, healthcare domain knowledge, and artificial intelligence** to solve practical problems in healthcare, life sciences, and enterprise environments.
